qgisMinimumVersion=3.34
qgisMaximumVersion=4.99
description=Will the survey see the target? Pre-survey detectability for gravity, magnetics and ground radar.
about=Describe a buried target and a survey; the plugin models the gravity, magnetic, magnetic-gradient and
    ground-radar response, compares it with instrument and field noise, and says whether the target is detectable,
    with the station spacing and the profile length. Click the target on the map, draw or pick the survey line, and
    add the stations along it as a point layer. The same is available as a Processing algorithm for a whole layer of
    lines. Every result lists its assumptions and the version of the library that computed it. Results are
    model-based expectations under the stated assumptions, not guarantees.
    No external dependency: the plugin carries the Detectability library (BSD-3-Clause) and uses the NumPy that
    comes with QGIS. It makes no network connection and collects no data.
